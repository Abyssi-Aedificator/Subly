Changelog

Version 1.2.0 -- 2026-11-25

- Schedule a subscription to switch from Suspended to Paused on its next renewal date. Suspended and active subscriptions share the same date order.
- Allow a custom icon URL to override a subscription favicon.
- Redesigned the app icon with a transparent background.


Version 1.1.0 -- 2026-11-24

- Show a service favicon when an optional website is saved and its icon is available.
- Automatically back up local subscription changes to Dropbox, with revision checks to protect newer backups.
- Show Dropbox connection, pending local changes, and transfer progress in Overview.
- Select a colour square in Appearance to apply a device-local palette across Subly.
- Keep Overview and Settings visible in a horizontal top menu, with a bottom accent and a left edge for Overview or right edge for Settings.


Version 1.0.0 -- 2026-11-23

- Refresh the installed app icon using new icon URLs so older cached artwork can be replaced.
- Keep service search and the status filter side by side.
- Show the app update date beside a stamp-style version badge.
- Keep the Subly logo and title static.
- Check for app updates manually from Settings.
- Choose whether to apply an available app update now or later.
- Use Roboto throughout the app, including offline.
- Redesigned Overview and Settings with a ledger layout and light and dark palettes.
- Added a ledger icon to the header, browser tab, and installed app.
- Add, edit, and delete online subscriptions with weekly, monthly, or yearly billing.
- View estimated monthly and annual costs in CAD and see the next three renewals.
- Search by service name and filter by Active, Paused, or Cancelled status.
- See active subscriptions in renewal order with countdowns, followed by paused and cancelled subscriptions with elapsed time based on their saved renewal dates.
- Choose Light, Dark, or System appearance in Settings.
- Back up and restore subscriptions manually through Dropbox.
- Install Subly as a web app and track subscriptions offline after the first online load.
